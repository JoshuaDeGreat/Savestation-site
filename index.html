Safety

This section is what stops people bouncing.

How HXSE works

  • Edits are queued until export (non-destructive workflow).
  • Export to a new file — keep an untouched original.
  • Post checksums and mirrors so people trust the download.

Disclaimer

Community tool. Not affiliated with Egosoft. Use at your own risk. If a patch breaks saves, keep a copy of the original and try older versions.

FAQ

Short, direct answers. No fluff.

Is HXSE Toolkit free?

Yes.

What save formats are supported?

.xml and .xml.gz imports, export as .xml.

Can I break my save?

Yes — that’s why you always keep an untouched backup and export to a new file.

Why “SaveStation”?

It’s the public hub: example saves, recipes, install help. HXSE Toolkit is the editor.
